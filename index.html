About Me

I am an M.Pharm graduate in Pharmaceutical Chemistry from M.S. Ramaiah University of Applied Science, Faculty of Pharmacy. My academic journey has been shaped by a deep passion for translating pharmaceutical and computational research into innovative therapeutic solutions.

With a strong foundation in pharmaceutical chemistry, I am committed to exploring the intersection of formulation science, computational approaches, and analytical research. My research interests span molecular docking, structure-activity relationships, ADMET prediction, and nanocarrier-based drug delivery systems.

I believe in combining traditional pharmaceutical research methodologies with cutting-edge computational tools to address real-world challenges in drug discovery and development. My goal is to contribute meaningfully to the pharmaceutical industry through rigorous research, innovative formulation strategies, and data-driven analytical approaches.

Analytical R&D

My focus is on supporting pharmaceutical research and development through comprehensive analytical approaches. This encompasses rigorous formulation evaluation, precise characterization of pharmaceutical systems, and research-oriented investigation of novel drug delivery mechanisms.

Analytical R&D involves leveraging advanced characterization techniques, stability testing methodologies, and data interpretation to ensure the efficacy, safety, and quality of pharmaceutical formulations. This expertise supports the entire drug development pipeline, from formulation optimization to pre-clinical evaluation.

Key Focus Areas

  • Formulation Characterization: Particle size, zeta potential, morphology analysis
  • Stability Assessment: Long-term and accelerated stability testing protocols
  • Drug Release Studies: In-vitro dissolution and release kinetics
  • Quality Control: Analytical method development and validation
  • Bioavailability Enhancement: Evaluation of nanocarrier systems and delivery mechanisms
